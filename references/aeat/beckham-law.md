## What the Regime Means

Article 93 lets qualifying individuals moving to Spain apply specified IRNR
rules while retaining IRPF taxpayer status (B1/B4). It is not ordinary
non-resident taxation and does not use ordinary Modelo 100 assumptions.

For a principal taxpayer it covers the tax period of residence acquisition and
the next five tax periods, subject to continued conditions (B1/B3). The period
is measured in tax years, not six rolling years from a Modelo 149 submission.
AEAT describes the first residence period for this regime by reference to the
first calendar year after displacement with more than 183 days in Spain.
Disputed residence or treaty questions require separate review; this is not a
shortcut for resolving every residence case.

## Entry Context

Before the 2023 reform the prior non-residence condition used ten tax periods;
the amended article 93 uses five preceding periods (B1/B2/B4). Determine which
rules govern the taxpayer's entry rather than replacing older entry conditions
because the return happens to be for 2025.

Post-reform pathways include qualifying employment (including qualifying
remote employment), administrator roles, qualifying entrepreneurial activity,
and certain highly qualified professionals/startup or training/R&D/innovation
activities, each subject to its own conditions (B1). Professional athletes'
special employment relationship is excluded. A visa, job in Spain, or employer
withholding at 24% is not enough to establish the option or continued status.

For administrators, the linked-entity participation restriction described in
article 93 applies when the entity is patrimonial; do not impose a universal
25% cap on all administrators. The permanent-establishment condition has
exceptions for the specified qualifying economic activities. Detect those
activities and refer them for review: the MVP does not implement their workflow.

Associated spouses/children, and the other parent without a marriage bond in
the specified case, have separate residence, arrival, age/disability, prior
non-residence and base-comparison conditions. Their period ends with the
principal's eligible period (B1/B4). Do not grant them six new years or assume
family-associated status solely from family relationship.

## Evidence to Request

- Arrival and activity-start dates, first Spanish residence tax year, and
  evidence relevant to prior residence and the claimed entry pathway.
- Modelo 149 submission acknowledgement and AEAT regime confirmation with
  applicable dates; submission alone is not continued-eligibility proof.
- Any renunciation, exclusion, end-of-displacement communication, and relevant
  changes in activity, residence, or associated-taxpayer conditions.
- Previous Modelo 151 returns as context, not conclusive proof of this year's
  status or valid mappings.

Keep the user's claim PROVIDED until supported; describe exactly which dates
and facts are verified. Escalate unresolved eligibility, late options, disputed
residence, family-associated conditions, or transitions. Do not pronounce a
binding approval or switch the user to Modelo 100 automatically.

## Modelo 149 vs Modelo 151

Modelo 149 communicates the option, renunciation, exclusion, or end of
displacement. Modelo 151 is the annual return under the special regime (B5/B6).
They are different procedures; neither substitutes for the other.

AEAT's current 149 procedure requires NIF/census identification and supporting
documentation submitted through its dedicated procedure before the option,
with the registration number included in the communication. Each taxpayer
communicates individually, principal before associated taxpayers (B5). The
MVP can explain and check evidence but must not upload or submit documents.

The usual principal option period is six months from the relevant activity
start evidence (Social Security or the applicable supporting documentation),
not always six months from arrival. Associated options and the December 2023
transition have their own rules. Renunciation normally concerns November and
December before the effective year; exclusion and end-of-displacement have
specific one-month communication rules (B3/B4/B6). Verify the exact event and
historical rule before advising on any deadline.

## Income and Other Obligations

Employment obtained during regime application, and qualifying entrepreneurial
activity income specified by article 93, is deemed obtained in Spain, subject
to the rule's temporal exceptions (B1/B4). Foreign payroll alone does not
exclude it. Work before displacement or after a communicated end needs a
separate temporal/source analysis, not blanket exclusion of all foreign pay.

Other receipts require their own Spanish-source analysis. A foreign bank or
broker is not by itself evidence of the source of the underlying income.
No worldwide inclusion/exclusion rule should replace source verification.

Article 93 expressly provides wealth-tax liability by **obligacion real**
(B1). Do not tell users that Beckham status eliminates wealth-tax obligations.
Assess filing thresholds/obligations separately with official advice; this
plugin does not prepare wealth-tax or informative returns, and must not import
generic Modelo 720/721 rules blindly.
